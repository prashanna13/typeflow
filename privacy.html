Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Summary

TypeFlow does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. Everything happens in your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, no analytics, and no third-party data sharing.

What Data is Stored

TypeFlow uses browser localStorage to save:

  • Completed lessons and scores
  • WPM and accuracy from tests and drills
  • Key-by-key accuracy statistics (used to generate weak-key exercises)
  • Streak count and practice time
  • Theme preference (dark, light, or system)
  • Achievements and game high scores

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to a server. It is not visible to us. It exists only in your browser's storage.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No email addresses or names
  • No IP addresses or device fingerprints
  • No usage analytics or session recordings
  • No cookies (except localStorage for progress)
  • No third-party tracking pixels or ad networks

Export and Import

You can export your progress as a JSON file and import it on another device. That file contains only the data listed above. Sharing that file with someone else gives them your statistics. Do not share it if you consider that information private.

Service Workers

TypeFlow may register a service worker to enable offline use and faster page loads. The service worker caches static files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts) locally. It does not track you or send data anywhere.

External Resources

TypeFlow loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google's privacy policy applies to those requests. We do not control or receive data from that service.

Children's Privacy

TypeFlow does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected or transmitted, COPPA compliance is straightforward: there is nothing to comply with.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will reflect that. Continued use means acceptance of the revised policy.
