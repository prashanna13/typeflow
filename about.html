About this site

TypeFlow is a free touch-typing school.

It teaches you from absolute zero — sitting position, hand placement, F and J bumps — through every letter, number, symbol, and the flow of real-world sentences. No accounts. No subscriptions. No premium gates. The full path is here.

TypeFlow runs entirely in this browser. Progress lives in local storage. Export a JSON file if you want a backup. No server. No tracking. Your weak-key statistics and streak count stay on your device unless you choose to share them by exporting the file.

Why another typing trainer?

Most typing sites throw beginners straight at the keyboard with no orientation. They skip the boring fundamentals and wonder why people quit after a week. TypeFlow starts where beginners actually are: confused about which finger to use, whether to look, and why anyone would bother learning this.

The hard part is not speed. The hard part is learning to trust a pattern you cannot see yet. TypeFlow builds that trust slowly, one key strip at a time, with enough repetition that the motion stops feeling theoretical.

Open source?

TypeFlow is a static site. View source if you are curious. Copy the structure if you want to build your own. The goal is a useful tool, not a guarded product.

Who made this?

A developer who got tired of recommending typing sites with paywalls and fake WPM inflation. If it helps someone build the skill, that is enough.
